03 / The photographer

About Francis Richards

From an early age, I have been fascinated by cameras and photography’s ability to preserve a moment, reveal a story and celebrate the world around us. My work has included weddings, sports, music festivals, cultural traditions, historic landmarks, wildlife and landscapes, from Dominica to the United States.

Serving as photo editor for the Wabash Valley College school yearbook strengthened my appreciation for thoughtful composition, careful selection and authentic visual storytelling. I continue to develop my craft while respecting the integrity of every original scene.
